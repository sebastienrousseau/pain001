# pain001 v0.0.69

In progress. The third example-corpus release planned in ADR-0003, plus
the pain.001 twin foundation of ADR-0005.

## Twin foundation (Phase A1)

- The ISO JSON twin of every pain.001 market file, in the ISO 20022 RA's
  2025 JSON convention, lossless in both directions, with a JSON Schema
  2020-12 per edition generated from the schema inventory.
- The records twin: the CSV pipeline's flat rows for any pain.001 file,
  with a measured list of what the pipeline cannot carry and which
  files it can regenerate today.
- Shipped twins and schemas within a 500 KB compressed budget; coverage
  twins on demand.
