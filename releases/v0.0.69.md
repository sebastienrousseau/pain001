# pain001 v0.0.69

In progress. The third example-corpus release planned in ADR-0003, plus
the pain.001 twin foundation of ADR-0005.

## Twin foundation (Phase A1)

- The ISO JSON twin of every pain.001 market file, in the ISO 20022 RA's
  2025 JSON convention, lossless in both directions, with a JSON Schema
  2020-12 per edition generated from the schema inventory.
- The records twin: the CSV pipeline's flat rows for any pain.001 file,
  with a measured list of what the pipeline cannot carry and which
  files it can regenerate today.
- Shipped twins and schemas within a 500 KB compressed budget; coverage
  twins on demand.
- The ISO 2018 compatibility face is deferred until the ISO 20022
  e-Repository is available for its name table.

## Tier 2 and 3 packs

- Seven scenarios across CZ, HK, SG, MY, QA and AE (Luxembourg shipped
  with the SEPA core pack): CERTIS with the Czech payment symbols, a
  Czech SEPA transfer, Hong Kong FPS, Singapore FAST, Malaysia DuitNow
  with a BNM purpose code, Qatar QATCH with the QCB purpose of payment,
  and UAE UAEFTS with the CBUAE regulatory reporting line. Every tier-3
  file carries the confidence chip `assumed` (ADR-0003, decision 8).

## CSV pipeline extension

- One flat vocabulary for the `.03` and `.09` to `.13` templates, about
  seventy columns documented in `docs/input-columns.md`: addresses,
  organisation ids, clearing member ids, account numbers with schemes,
  payment type, purpose, regulatory reporting, structured creditor
  references, ultimate parties, UETR, and a date-time execution date.
  An account is an IBAN or a number; an agent a BIC or a clearing
  member. 68 of the 70 pain.001 market files regenerate through the
  pipeline exactly; the cheque scenario is the documented exception.
