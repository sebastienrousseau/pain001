# pain001 v0.0.68

In progress. The second example-corpus release planned in ADR-0003: the
tier-1 market packs (UK, the SEPA core countries, US, CH, SE) as
scenarios with provenance, HSBC overlays where the evidence exists, and
the corpus surfaced on the website and through the MCP and LSP tools.

## UK market pack (first tier-1 pack)

- Five scenarios: Faster Payments single, Bacs Direct Credit supplier run,
  Bacs Direct Debit collection, CHAPS property completion, international
  USD from a UK account; .03 and .09 (pain.008 .02 and .08 for the debit).
- HSBC variants for each rail from the bank's HSBCnet guidelines, built
  through overlay patches and judged by the overlays' rules; the guideline
  exports stay outside the repository and a compliance test enforces it.
- The plan's open question is closed: HSBC UK Faster Payments carries the
  service level `URNS`.
- Twenty UK files, ten of them HSBC variants; the data tree is 260 KB of
  the 400 KB budget.

## SEPA core pack

- Thirteen scenarios across DE, FR, NL, BE, ES, IT and LU: credit transfers
  with each country's reference convention, SEPA Instant, Core and B2B
  direct debits with the national creditor identifiers, and the German
  urgent-euro and foreign-payment order types.
- HSBC SEPA variants from the HSBCnet Europe guidelines, plus four public
  country overlays from the national implementation guides.
- Overlays now declare the editions they cover, so a v03 guideline never
  judges a .09 file.

## US pack

- Seven scenarios: ACH CCD and PPD credits, an ACH debit, the EFTPS
  federal tax deposit with the TXP addenda, a Fedwire transfer, an RTP
  payment and a bank-issued cheque, with HSBC US variants for ACH, wire,
  real-time and debit from the HSBCnet North America guidelines.

## Swiss pack

- Four scenarios following the Swiss Payment Standards payment types:
  a QR-bill settlement (QR-IBAN and QRR reference), a domestic transfer
  with an ISO 11649 SCOR reference, a SEPA credit transfer from a Swiss
  account and a cross-border USD payment, with HSBC Switzerland variants
  for low value and TARGET/international payments.

## Swedish pack

- Four scenarios: a Bankgiro supplier payment with OCR references, a
  Plusgiro payment, a Bankgirot Löner salary batch and an urgent SEK
  payment through RIX, with HSBC Sweden Virtual Presence variants for
  low value and domestic high value payments.

## Coverage files you can pick by name

- The schema coverage files are named for what they exercise
  (`01-transfer-every-element.xml`, `05-transfer-UltmtDbtr-PstlAdr-Id.xml`)
  instead of `set-NN.xml`, and `coverage.json` carries a manifest with a
  one-sentence description of each file.

## Overlay scoping

- Country overlays now name the scenarios they judge instead of a rail
  family, so a UK or German guideline no longer judges a US, Swiss or
  Swedish file. The tree is 35 scenarios and 204 market files (32 HSBC
  variants) at 373 KB of the 400 KB budget.

Sections are filled in as each pack lands.
