# pain001 v0.0.68

In progress. The second example-corpus release planned in ADR-0003: the
tier-1 market packs (UK, the SEPA core countries, US, CH, SE) as
scenarios with provenance, HSBC overlays where the evidence exists, and
the corpus surfaced on the website and through the MCP and LSP tools.

## UK market pack (first tier-1 pack)

- Five scenarios: Faster Payments single, Bacs Direct Credit supplier run,
  Bacs Direct Debit collection, CHAPS property completion, international
  USD from a UK account; .03 and .09 (pain.008 .02 and .08 for the debit).
- HSBC variants for each rail from the bank's HSBCnet guidelines, built
  through overlay patches and judged by the overlays' rules; the guideline
  exports stay outside the repository and a compliance test enforces it.
- The plan's open question is closed: HSBC UK Faster Payments carries the
  service level `URNS`.
- Twenty UK files, ten of them HSBC variants; the data tree is 260 KB of
  the 400 KB budget.

Sections are filled in as each pack lands.
